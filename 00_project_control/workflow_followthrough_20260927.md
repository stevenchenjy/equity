# Scoped blockers and recurring research work

Effective after verified deployment of the September 27, 2026 implementation.
The owner explicitly requested these four workflow changes. This document
does not change allocation targets, risk limits, cash reserve, trading
authority, or the experimental status of the momentum adaptation.

Subsequent explicit owner approval on the same date removes the mandatory
internal cash reserve. See `account_state_policy.md`: the reserve floor is
zero, no additional reserve asset is recorded, and existing allocation targets
and position risk limits remained unchanged by that September reserve change.
The subsequent [October 6 allocation policy](allocation_policy.md) supersedes
the earlier asset targets and fixed name cap; this workflow follows the current
approved policy rather than preserving the old numerical profile.

## Blocker scope

Account integrity, unresolved executions, unbounded buy commitments, invalid
source ledgers, and failed shared data gates block all new capital. An
incomplete or stale order inventory cannot establish that buying power is
uncommitted. Freshly observed sell-only orders reserve the named holding;
no sale proceeds or released shares are assumed. Contradictory orders and
oversubscribed holdings remain account integrity failures.

Expired plans, quotes, missing company research, new earnings, valuation,
and issuer-news assessments are attached to their ticker. Tactical budget
uncertainty remains attached to the tactical strategy. Those issues cannot
independently freeze an otherwise eligible unrelated core or growth idea.
Every candidate still must pass its original strategy, evidence, account,
whole-share and risk gates. The overlay only filters existing eligibility;
it does not manufacture a proposal from a lower-level diagnostic quantity.

`workflow_integrity.global_blockers`, `ticker_blockers`, and
`strategy_blockers` record these scopes. Legacy `blockers` remains the global
alias. `new_capital_allowed` means no workflow-wide block, not that any
particular security is suitable or execution-ready.

## Cash and recurring work

The daily decision carries a cash explanation and a durable work queue.
Planning cash is split into retained reserve and unallocated research cash.
Reasons for nondeployment overlap and are never added as dollar buckets.
The owner's capital measure can include outside-account cash; it does not
establish broker buying power, settlement, or a completed transfer.

The queue carries stable identities, first observation, current blockers,
next step, source bindings, suggested review time and change history. It
retains vanished, unsuccessful, expired and superseded items without calling
them successful. A corrupt history remains preserved and is reported as
unverified. The next automatic check follows existing scheduler slots;
actual execution depends on the host and successful collection. Suggested
owner review appointments are not additional automated jobs or entry windows.

## Bounded objective research

The existing full refresh runs the backlog step after SEC artifact collection
and before earnings incorporation, baseline generation and valuation. It
selects at most three issuers per run using held/reopened evidence and the
existing fundamental research queue. Three is a processing budget, not an
investment rule. Completed attempts and unchanged source sets are retained,
so lower-priority work can advance without restarting research each day.

Objective completion attaches verified numerical evidence from retained
official source receipts. The explicit scheduled apply mode can supplement
missing same-period financial fields only when source identity, hashes,
acceptance times, units, consolidation and existing values agree. Existing
values and scope cannot be silently revised. Subsequent earnings,
valuation and thesis gates still apply. Ambiguous or missing data remains
unknown; no forecast, multiple, thesis signoff or investment purpose is
generated by the objective worker. Its public collection is supplied by
the existing collectors; this step purchases nothing and makes no requests.

## Expired plans and reporting

Expired, failed, missed and review-due plans enter a separate reassessment
queue bound to their original plan ID, version and record hash. Original
purpose and deadlines remain visible. Completion requires reconciled facts
and a recorded sourced reassessment; changing purpose continues to use the
existing position-purpose evidence requirements. Old DAY drafts and prices
are never renewed merely by refreshing the queue.

Daily artifacts show progress even when no trade qualifies. Scheduled email
retains its current meaningful-change policy. Queue timestamps and ordinary
ranking movement do not create repeat mail. A newly invalid account-wide
order inventory remains a material gate change. Eligible core additions
for already-held ETFs are rendered and compared from their candidate rows,
separately from the retained holding quantity.

Software acceptance covers blocker isolation, corrupt history, point-in-time
source validation, repeated runs, reporting and pipeline order. These checks
do not demonstrate investment returns, improved capital efficiency or a
profitable momentum adaptation. The existing forward experiment remains
the separate performance-evidence track.
