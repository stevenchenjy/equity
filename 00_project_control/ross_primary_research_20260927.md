## Documented practices and the limits of importing them

**R08 — [Bull Flag Chart Pattern & Trading Strategies](https://www.warriortrading.com/bull-flag-trading/), updated October 22, 2023, Ross Cameron.** Ross primarily uses 1- and 5-minute charts. He looks for strong advances on elevated volume, quieter consolidation near highs, then volume-supported continuation. Entry is the next candle breaking the preceding pullback candle's high; the pullback low defines failure. Warning examples include retracing over half the advance, heavier consolidation volume, or losing VWAP/9EMA. He describes a 2:1 potential profit/loss requirement, partial profits, and exiting promptly when follow-through fails or tape/Level 2 weakens. Flat-top breakouts consolidate immediately below highs. The article allows daily-chart flags but says longer holding requires more fundamental analysis and earnings can override the chart. Its asserted statistical edge is not independently validated here. **Implication:** prior highs are context for a conditional entry, not an automatic rejection or an automatic buy; longer holds require additional evidence.

**R09 — [2025 Small Account Tool Kit, version “1013”](https://cdn.warriortrading.com/warriortrading.com/assets/2025-Small-Account-Tool-Kit-1013.pdf), labeled 2025; exact publication/revision date unverified.** Pages 1–4 and 8 describe differing contexts: $1–20 general price preference versus $5–10 for a leverage-constrained challenge; RVOL ≥5 and already up ≥10% (with a continuation exception); float below 20 million, tightening to 10 million in a cold market; positive catalyst/top gapper preference; 1/5-minute pullbacks; early-session attention. Example risk rules include $50 per trade, $100 daily loss and three consecutive losses, while a worksheet uses approximately 5% account risk and 10% daily loss. These are his examples, not safe universal limits. The document mentions international-broker platform fees often exceeding $200/month plus commissions/wires. It advocates quick profits and reducing activity in weak conditions. Its RVOL example uses a 30-day average. **Decision:** preserve the concepts of selectivity, explicit failure and sizing; do not import these numeric price/float/risk thresholds or broker assumptions into production.

**R10 — [Watch List of the Best Stocks for Day Trading](https://www.warriortrading.com/day-trading-watch-list-top-stocks-to-watch/), undated live page, retrieved September 27, 2026.** The public scanner explicitly says 15-minute delay. The page defines both a recent 5-minute volume measure and a daily ratio against a 14-day average, differing from R09. It describes news/price/float as screening properties of volatile trading candidates, not business quality. These definitions are not interchangeable. **Implication:** record numerator, denominator, bar interval, session, provider and observation time; an end-of-day ratio must never be represented as live, same-time-of-day RVOL.

**R11 — [Routing, Hot Keys, and Buying/Selling Process](https://support.warriortrading.com/support/solutions/articles/19000045007-routing-hot-keys-and-buying-selling-process), updated July 22, 2026.** The support description uses limit orders, fast hotkeys, Level 2 checks, five-cent order offsets and actively watched mental stops. The 10–20-cent loss and 20–40-cent gain examples assume immediate attention and execution. **Decision:** do not emulate mental stops in an unattended research system or assume a daily bar close reproduces these fills. Entry validity must be checked again at delivery/execution, with stale ideas expired or explicitly reassessed.

**R12 — [Verified Day Trading Earnings](https://www.warriortrading.com/ross-camerons-verified-day-trading-earnings/), retrieved September 27, 2026; stated audit period 2017–2025.** This is the provider's index of personal results and an accountant's report. It expressly says results are not typical, customer outcomes are not tracked, and profit figures may be gross or net. It is not a benchmark for this system. No headline dollar figure is used here as evidence of transferable performance.

## Proposed workspace adaptations (our design, not Ross's rules)

These proposals require the main workspace audit to establish actual data and architecture. The principles are justified as research controls; their investment benefit remains a testable hypothesis.

1. **Add independent momentum evidence to existing research.** Timestamp catalysts and verify their original source; annotate price strength, prior highs, explicitly defined volume ratios, float provenance and liquidity. Keep business quality, financing/dilution and valuation in separate fields. Unknown information stays unknown. Low price or float must not upgrade a business-quality assessment.
2. **Give each idea a strategy and lifecycle.** Separate intraday momentum, multi-day trend and longer-term growth. Record setup, trigger, acceptable entry range, failure condition, expiry, horizon, evidence freshness and purpose. Preserve expired/failed/missed ideas and make a later thesis a new recorded reassessment linked to the old one.
3. **Respect data and attention limits.** Where only daily data or scheduled research is verified, produce daily research candidates and next-session reassessment needs. Minute-pattern detection, live tape/depth, real-time breakout confirmation and fill claims remain unverified. A timer is not proof that a signal reaches the owner while actionable.
4. **Use existing approved risk as a binding cap.** A proposed position size must fit entry-to-failure distance, conservative cost/gap assumptions, available settled cash and existing portfolio constraints. Stop price is a scenario, not a guaranteed fill. Exposure reductions may be proposed when original evidence deteriorates; no limit is relaxed or allocation changed by this research.
5. **Freeze and compare an experimental overlay.** Record the existing approach and experiment version before observing outcomes. Compare baseline and overlay at the same timestamp/universe/capital constraints, after spread, commissions, slippage and missed fills. Retain rejected, failed, skipped, expired and missed opportunities. Use chronological unseen or forward observations; avoid selection using today's survivors or news unavailable at decision time. Report uncertainty and adverse regimes. Do not retune after a short streak.
6. **Keep software and investment evidence separate.** Schema/tests, repeatable jobs and honest unknowns can establish reliability. They cannot establish alpha. Forward observations should report both policy compliance/actionability and outcome evidence, without promotion to approved strategy until a documented review and any required owner approval.

Deferred until independently feasible and validated: unattended intraday scalping; automatic use of low-float/$1–20 screens; 5× RVOL and 10% momentum as universal gates; challenge-sized risk; leverage or offshore-broker migration; paid data/tools; automatic strategy promotion; converting a failed scalp into a growth holding. A daily-chart adaptation is a distinct experiment, even though Ross discusses daily flags.
