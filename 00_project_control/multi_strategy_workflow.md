failed. A favorable price setup does not establish business quality, a profitable
method, executable entry or permission to allocate. Keep these findings in their
own route. New production entry authority or a longer actual tactical holding
limit requires the exact strategy/policy review and explicit owner adoption.

The existing $4,000 planning basis, zero mandatory reserve, combined SPY/QQQM
core minimum, aggregate stock maximum, absence of fixed single-name growth caps,

## One explanation of existing decisions

`decision_readiness.py` provides the optional
`equity_strategy_framework_v1` read-only projection after the existing capital
contract is composed. It validates that contract and its current source bindings.
It does not create a second recommender or enlarge a quantity. The dashboard
shows the original canonical outcome and one explanation class:

- `valid_action`: an exact current admitted conditional draft; manual review and
  execution checks remain necessary.
- `justified_no_action`: a completed current hold/rejection or observed economic
  failure. An unchanged dossier is not a reason to repeat it.
- `research_incomplete`: current business, valuation, countercase or mandate
  synthesis is unfinished; the recurring analyst owns a sourced conclusion.
- `plan_expired`: a dated instruction elapsed. Preserve its original record and
  deadline; use a real reassessment rather than automatic renewal.
- `data_unavailable`: source/current-publication, required-close or actual
  account/order evidence is unavailable or invalid. Keep the exact dependency.
- `strategy_not_adopted`: a strategy requires its own reviewed adoption; no
  amount of presentation or collector progress clears it.

An invalid/stale publication withholds projected draft prices and quantities.
A valid maintained exit can remain independently assessable when unrelated
new-capital market or company gates fail. Historical negative conclusions remain
visible as dated evidence even when a newer required close is unavailable.

## Autonomous analyst continuity

The bounded `analyst_handoff` combines the existing plan/capital work queue,
capital dependency owners and validated research-opportunity context. It names
the next task, resolver, deadline, accepted record identities and source IDs.
The bound controls the next useful batch, not final decision coverage or a
minimum number of investment conclusions.

At each existing full review or admitted short assessment, inspect that handoff
alongside the current authoritative queues. Urgent held/adverse risk and imminent
events retain priority. Continue a precise unfinished case; source collection,
an attached dossier or a software refresh is not completed analysis. Use existing
thesis/model/plan/attention writers for any actual amendment and the full no-send
publication procedure after accepted analyst changes. Preserve original sources,
failed attempts, timestamps, record ancestry and completed negative decisions.

Approved local-ledger records support conditional research. A research deadline
alone does not require a new broker snapshot or owner-authored investment case.
Actual account/order contradictions and unresolved earlier instructions retain
their owner-observation requirement. Before any manual submission, executable
funds, remaining shares, pending orders and unrecorded changes still need checking.

The new research projections run in existing processes; they add no research
slot, trade, sender or email purpose. Existing mandatory briefs and duplicate /
unknown-send controls remain unchanged.

## Effectiveness and adoption

Distinguish correct software, completed source-bound research, plausible strategy
logic and demonstrated performance. A scenario price gap, score, admitted company
model or passed refresh does not establish a win probability or investment edge.
Experimental outcomes retain first-observation clocks, missed entries, failed
setups, unfavorable paths, cost/slippage assumptions and missing coverage.
Compare fixed versions on unseen/forward evidence; do not retune after a streak
or choose a favorable historical version retrospectively. Only an exact reviewed
strategy adoption can authorize new capital behavior.
