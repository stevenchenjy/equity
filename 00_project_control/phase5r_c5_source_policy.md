# Phase 5R-C5 Source Policy

## Source Priority

1. Company investor-relations releases and presentations.
2. SEC 10-K, 10-Q, and 8-K filings.
3. Official earnings releases.
4. Public read-only market data.
5. Reputable financial reporting when primary evidence is unavailable.

Social-media promotion, anonymous claims, and popularity signals are not research evidence.

## Controlled Notes

The company packet builder contains a reviewed evidence registry with source URLs, checked dates, concise observations, and explicit component scores. It does not scrape arbitrary pages during a weekly run. Current-position technical context may use yfinance public data when available and fails to an unavailable status without inventing values.

The weekly deep-research queue admits a B2 candidate only when that ticker already has a controlled evidence packet. An unsupported market mover remains in B2 output until a reviewed primary-source packet is added.

## Data Boundaries

- Canonical C4/C4R and B2 inputs only.
- Current IOT/RBRK state only from the Git-ignored local positions file.
- No archived folders, credentials, broker data, SMTP configuration, or private account services.
- Source uncertainty is retained in notes and lowers confidence where appropriate.
