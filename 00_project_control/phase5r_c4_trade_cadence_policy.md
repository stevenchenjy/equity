# Phase 5R-C4 Trade Cadence Policy

## Cadence

- Generate new buy candidates weekly, not daily.
- Default to zero to two new buy-review candidates per week.
- Review the portfolio once per week.
- Review rebalancing and concentration once per month.
- Allow waiting with no action when evidence or price is not compelling.

## Prohibited Rhythm

- No day trading.
- No every-15-minute scan.
- No repeated intraday emails or notifications.
- No daily scheduler while D1 is parked.
- No automatic buying, selling, adding, trimming, or exiting.

## Event Handling

A future event-driven workflow may surface major thesis, filing, governance, or risk changes. Routine price noise and ordinary volatility do not qualify. Event handling is not implemented in C4.
