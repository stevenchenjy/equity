# Phase 5R-C1 Low-Attention Rules

1. Compose one daily brief concept only.
2. Do not create an intraday alert, recurring scheduler, repeated notification, or every-15-minute scan.
3. Keep the body scannable: one market-status summary, up to three manual-review candidates, up to five watch entries, and up to three lower-priority examples.
4. Use research language: `Manual-review candidate`, `Watch`, and `Lower priority / avoid today`.
5. State that public market data may be delayed and that every action requires independent human review.
6. State that the system cannot connect to a broker or place orders.
7. Produce local preview artifacts only. C1 has no sending or delivery capability.
