# Research and owner collaboration

Effective: 2026-09-04 reassessment implementation. This document governs
research presentation, not portfolio policy or authority.

The owner supplies objectives, investment horizon, cash requirements, risk
tolerance, and actual manual fills/funding. Natural-language quantity, price
and net proceeds are acceptable; missing facts are not silently assumed zero.
The owner does not label every model run or operate routine review templates.

The assistant maintains authorized workflows, offers a reasoned independent
view and the strongest counterargument, and reports four things first: what
changed, whether it changes the thesis, the main remaining uncertainty, and
the next decisive evidence. Implementation status and engineering metrics are
supporting information, not proof of investment usefulness.

Company research follows hypothesis → official evidence delta → conditional
price-implied expectations → deterministic portfolio constraints → subsequent
verification. Maintain a small number of falsifiable questions, distinguish
facts from management claims and estimates, and do not repeat unchanged risks
as new discoveries. A stock score cannot replace business, valuation and
portfolio reasoning. Missing company-specific assumptions remain missing.

The deterministic research companion runs within the existing capital
validation reporting step and writes only noncanonical research artifacts.
It uses no model and is not read by the decision engine or sender. Reverse
valuation sensitivities hold net debt and shares fixed, do not include interim
distributions, and are not forecasts, DCFs, expected total returns, or trading
instructions. Existing revenue-multiple gates remain an explicit heuristic.

Whole-share diagnostics separate cash affordability, target headroom, default
position limits, hard caps and turnover. Alternative ±1-share weights are
research scenarios only. No tolerance band, risk line, core timing policy or
notification eligibility changes merely to make a candidate pass.

Forward close-based outcome measurement begins at the next observed session
after the snapshot's aware creation date. This conservative date-only rule
avoids backdating and early-close ambiguity. Original snapshots and the
pre-correction derived outcome file are preserved. One primary observation per
ticker/origin is fixed by creation time and ID, never by later performance.
Overlapping horizons and correlated securities are not independent trials.
Missing corporate-action, distribution and portfolio-cash history prevents
claims of net total return, causal performance or validated outperformance.

“Assess” means inspect and propose; “implement” authorizes the bounded change
and verification. Routine authorized operations can complete autonomously.
Changes to investment assumptions, production authority, or hard risk policy
remain explicit architecture-level choices. SHADOW remains read-only,
noncanonical, and unable to affect decisions, notifications or execution.
