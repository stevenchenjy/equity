# Phase 5R-C4 Concentration Policy

## Default Limits

- Single-stock default cap: `6%`.
- High-conviction single-stock hard cap: `8%`.
- Active stock sleeve cap: `30%`.
- Technology exposure default cap: `60%`.
- AI infrastructure theme cap: `35%`.
- Cash reserve target: `10%`.
- New idea default starting size: `2%` to `4%`.
- Watch-only idea suggested size: `0%`.

## Application Rules

- No position may be increased without checking all available concentration limits.
- A position above `6%` requires explicit high-conviction review and documentation.
- A position may not exceed `8%` under the default framework.
- New ideas should normally begin between `2%` and `4%`; starting size does not waive portfolio-level caps.
- Technology and AI-infrastructure limits require sector/theme classification in a later enrichment phase; C4 reports them as not evaluable from the minimum private position schema.
- Breaches trigger `trim_review` or `wait` analysis, not automatic transactions.
