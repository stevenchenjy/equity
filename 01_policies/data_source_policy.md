# Data Source Policy

Use primary sources whenever possible and keep source quality visible in every memo, screen, and report.

## Preferred Sources

- SEC filings and public SEC data.
- Company investor relations pages and official press releases.
- Exchange filings, notices, and listing information.
- FDA pages and official regulatory communications.
- Official government sources.
- Audited financial statements.

## Secondary Sources

- Established financial data providers.
- Reputable financial news organizations.
- Earnings call transcript providers.
- Industry reports with clear publisher identity and methodology.

## Weak Evidence

- Social media.
- Forums.
- Blogs.
- Influencer posts.
- Promotional newsletters.
- Unsourced screenshots.

Weak evidence may suggest research questions, but it must not drive a trade plan unless confirmed by preferred sources.

## Prohibited Sources And Data

- Broker account data.
- Bank, debit card, credit card, password, API key, cookie, token, or login information.
- Paid APIs added without explicit review.
- Any data source that requires storing sensitive credentials in this repo.
