# Phase 5R-C6 Weekly Email Policy

## Purpose

Phase 5R-C6 composes one low-attention weekly conviction brief from canonical C5 and C5T outputs. The primary decision is `no_action_until_next_review`.

## Decision Hierarchy

1. State the no-action primary plan first.
2. Review IOT and RBRK as current local positions and retain their `trim_review` labels because of concentration.
3. Explain why waiting is selected for this week.
4. Show only the light 25% and whole-share backup scenarios.
5. Summarize new candidates and selected next-review triggers.

The stricter 8% and 6% scenarios may be mentioned as policy references, but they are not the selected plan.

## Attention Boundary

- One weekly brief, designed to be understood in under two minutes.
- No daily watchlist delivery, repeated notification, or time-sensitive alert behavior.
- No pressure to change the portfolio before the next review on `2026-07-16`.
- Every future portfolio decision remains independent and manual.

## Data Boundary

IOT and RBRK are read only from the current Git-ignored local positions file and canonical C5/C5T outputs. Archived holding records are not inputs.
