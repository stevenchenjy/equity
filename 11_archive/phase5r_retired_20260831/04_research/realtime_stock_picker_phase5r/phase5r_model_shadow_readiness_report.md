# Phase 5R Model Shadow Readiness — Research Summary

Date: 2026-07-28 ET

The deterministic daily system remains the research authority. The model layer
is locally hardened but disabled.

## Selected research design

- Zero model calls for no-change and insufficient-evidence cycles.
- Terra is the evidence-interpretation baseline.
- Luna is a lower-cost blinded analyst comparison, not an assumed replacement.
- Sol is reserved for possible classification changes and high-impact
  criticism.
- A cross-provider challenger is deferred unless issuer-held-out results show
  correlated same-family errors.
- Public market data is context only; valuation-sensitive transitions require
  stronger ticker-scoped evidence or abstain.

The model can classify research as reject, watchlist, hold, paper-trade
candidate, real-trade candidate, trim review, exit review, or abstain. C9 and
the deterministic adjudicator then enforce a separate action-review ceiling.
No model can send an email, connect to a broker, create an order, or authorize
execution.
