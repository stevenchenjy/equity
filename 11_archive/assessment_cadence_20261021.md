# Assessment cadence adoption — October 21, 2026

The owner requested two full reviews and two daily briefs even without action,
plus 90-minute short assessments during the regular open session. Extra emails
may report material eligible-action changes or substantive analyst research
priority changes; they need not imply a cleared allocation. Deployment and
future delivery are separate verification claims.

The adopted contract takes effect October 22. Full reviews remain 08:45 and
13:45 Eastern. Short slots are 09:30, 11:00, 12:30, 14:00 and 15:30 Eastern,
subject to the existing exchange calendar, early closes, bounded admission and
no catch-up. The two existing daily delivery windows remain unchanged.

The active definition is [assessment_cadence.md](../00_project_control/assessment_cadence.md).
Private release evidence is retained in the production checkout under
`08_reviews/owner_requested.local/assessment-cadence-20261021/`.

Only delivery purpose and research cadence change. Valuations, portfolio and
risk limits, entry criteria, experimental status, account facts and human-only
execution remain authoritative. No trade or test email is authorized by this
adoption. The unchanged legacy notification signal is distinct from the new
mandatory brief purpose; a required status-only brief cannot recycle stale
orders or claim that a failed research handoff passed.
