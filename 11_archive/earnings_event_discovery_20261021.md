# Independent earnings-event research — October 21, 2026

The former research adapters admitted only the momentum shortlist and configured
issuer news. A publicly scheduled reporting event outside those routes could
remain absent from research. The owner approved a general earnings-event route,
with unchanged entry and risk requirements. Adding one issuer would not repair
the discovery cause.

The new bounded public calendar stage precedes intake. It retains every valid
returned lead and actual observation time over the configured forward horizon;
Nasdaq dates remain estimates and coverage remains provider/date specific.
Immutable raw, policy and scan receipts preserve duplicates, conflicts, failed
dates and original first detection. An old notice fetched after its event is
not described as pre-event discovery.

Deadline-aware attention can displace ordinary nonheld work and reserve the
existing bounded external objective slots after urgent held work. It does not
raise total research/network budgets or turn a dossier into analyst completion.
An explicit event assessment records expectations, priced-in thesis, valuation,
downside and recheck conditions against the detected source before its deadline.
The production capital engine remains the eligibility owner. Valuations,
allocation, numerical risk controls and human-only execution are unchanged.

The scheduled text/HTML and actual dashboard expose a separate earnings research
view, with estimated schedule, research deadline, review status and dependencies.
Upcoming work precedes elapsed history; the full dashboard list is paginated
rather than silently truncated. Recorded negative or unresolved judgments remain
visible. Publication validation rechecks nested source bytes and current event
semantics; unrelated source failure can be disclosed on recomposition without
becoming an account-wide investment restriction. Stable completed advice can
participate in the existing delivery comparison; calendar/refetch churn cannot
create filler delivery. No schedule, recipient or execution authority changed.

The observed omission regression fails with the original trigger adapter: two
synthetic weak-momentum outside-universe companies do not reach research. It
passes after repair. Additional checks cover capacity, shared budgets, stale and
conflicting schedule invalidation, review-source durability across unrelated row
changes, late/backdated admission, raw corruption, partial coverage, source-bound
zero-entry outcomes, email separation and responsive paginated display.

Versioned production adapter bindings advance for changed implementations only.
No approved numerical policy or strategy authority changes. Deployment, exact
test totals, real pre-event observations, full refresh, final artifacts and
no-send/no-trade evidence belong in the dated private production completion
receipt; this source record is not itself deployment or delivery proof.
