# Memo Rubric

Score company memos out of 100. A score below 70 should stay out of paper trade candidates.

## Scoring

### Business Clarity - 15 Points

- 0-5: Business model is vague or unsupported.
- 6-10: Basic business model is understandable.
- 11-15: Product, customer, value proposition, and business stage are clear.

### Growth Evidence - 15 Points

- 0-5: Growth claim is unsupported or stale.
- 6-10: Growth is supported by some reported data.
- 11-15: Growth evidence is current, sourced, and tied to filings or official releases.

### Balance Sheet - 15 Points

- 0-5: Cash, debt, burn, or runway are missing.
- 6-10: Balance sheet basics are present but incomplete.
- 11-15: Cash, debt, burn, runway, and financing needs are clearly sourced.

### Quality Of Evidence - 15 Points

- 0-5: Thesis relies on weak sources.
- 6-10: Mix of primary and secondary sources.
- 11-15: Key facts come from SEC filings, official company materials, exchange filings, FDA pages, or official government sources.

### Valuation - 15 Points

- 0-5: No valuation framing.
- 6-10: Basic valuation context is present.
- 11-15: Valuation risk and downside scenarios are clearly addressed.

### Catalyst And Timing - 10 Points

- 0-3: Catalyst timing is missing.
- 4-7: Known catalysts are listed.
- 8-10: Catalysts, timing, and event risks are sourced and connected to the thesis.

### Liquidity And Tradability - 15 Points

- 0-5: Liquidity, spread, float, and tradability are missing.
- 6-10: Basic liquidity check is present.
- 11-15: Dollar volume, tradability constraints, and exit feasibility are clearly assessed.

## Interpretation

- 90-100: Strong research memo, still not a trade recommendation.
- 80-89: Good memo with minor gaps.
- 70-79: Adequate for paper-trade consideration if red-team review and risk calculation are complete.
- Below 70: Stay out of paper trade candidates.

Any missing memo, missing red-team note, missing risk calculation, or thesis based only on Tier 3 sources is an automatic stop.
