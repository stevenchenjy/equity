## Corrections for any later noncanonical use

### 1. ARM operating-margin direction

Affected review row:
`ab5b5abbd47bb74c8befb95f04915ce5d75498962693d46d59e99e8ddd678e47`.

The source supports revenue and operating-income growth, but not an
unqualified operating-margin improvement in the latest annual comparison.
Operating margin was 3% in 2024, 21% in 2025, and 18% in 2026. A corrected
formulation is:

> Revenue and operating income rose across the annual comparison; operating
> margin improved relative to 2024 but declined from 2025 to 2026.

### 2. Broadcom tender-offer pricing scope

Affected review row:
`af95fdf118586c77bc04b6f9e7ecc6f9dde943ebf9b6ca372860d1a14b51555c`.

The supplied 8-K states that attached press releases announced pricing terms,
expiration, upsize, and results for debt tender offers. It does not itself show
the pricing terms. A corrected formulation is:

> The supplied 8-K reports that attached press releases announced tender-offer
> pricing terms and the expiration, upsize, and results; the excerpt itself
> does not disclose the specific terms.

### 3. PANW cash-flow period-to-citation binding

Affected review row:
`400146d357c3b58b9b7868439b97eefad8b08a8cb5b9f3c8d8f6b12bbd6915c9`.

The claim's cited excerpt reports the cash-flow figures but does not clearly
bind them to the asserted nine-month period. The explicit nine-month
reconciliation exists only in a separate critic evidence excerpt. A corrected
formulation must either cite that reconciliation directly or avoid asserting
the period:

> In the separately cited nine-month reconciliation, operating cash flow and
> management-defined free cash flow were higher year over year.

### 4. Micron customer-concentration scope

Affected review row:
`bb598fd8ea50d4dca4ab69c7efa4dbfbc80b6caacca9cf8dab548321f3cbe05f`.

The source says that one customer accounted for 10% of revenue in the current
nine-month period and 16% in the prior-year period. It does not identify that
customer as the largest or establish an overall dependence conclusion. A
corrected formulation is:

> The disclosed customer's share of revenue declined from 16% to 10%; the
> supplied excerpt does not establish whether it was the largest customer or
> whether overall customer dependence changed.
